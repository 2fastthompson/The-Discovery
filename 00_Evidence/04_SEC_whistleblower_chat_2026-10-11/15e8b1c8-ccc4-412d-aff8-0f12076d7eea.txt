- **Safety monitoring without copying the whole corpus**
- **Lower data exposure**
- **Cross-system auditing**
- **Evidence that an intervention happened before output**
- **A portable record for investigating failures**

But it generally cannot prove that the answer was correct unless the trace includes enough evidence to independently verify the claims. The strongest design is a **minimal evidence trace**: not the full corpus, but the exact retrieved passages—or cryptographic commitments to them—needed to validate each generated claim.
